v0.2: The Lock

Stop forbidden beliefs at the door, instead of throwing them out afterwards

← Back to the main recommendation
Today (v0.1.1)
A rite adopts “divorce allowed”→“Rite Changed” banner→Guard undoes it, up to a month later

Correct in the end, but noisy, and sometimes costly: lost fervor, wasted piety.

With v0.2
The forbidden choice is refused or undone on the spot→No banner, no cost

The guard stays as a safety net for anything that still slips through.

Why not simply hide forbidden options everywhere? Shadows of the Cross rewrites the rules of most doctrines and tenets. If we rewrote them too, we would erase its changes. So, as with the bulls, we hook the few game steps every change passes through. Each hook below is a base-game step that SOTC calls but doesn’t rewrite. I checked each one in the game files.

1The six doors, and what v0.2 does at each

1

The AI’s yearly drift

Safe hook
WhatEach year a rite’s AI leader may adopt a new doctrine or tenet. This is the main source of the banners you saw.
TodayAdopted, announced by letter, fervor lost; then the guard reverts it.
v0.2Undone the instant it is picked, before any letter or fervor loss. The rite simply doesn’t drift that year.
2

New rites springing up

Safe hook
WhatBig rites sometimes split, and the game builds a new rite with fresh differences.
TodayThe new rite may be born with forbidden beliefs, cleaned at the next monthly check.
v0.2Cleaned right after it is built, before it spreads to any county.
3

The AI Pope’s choices

Safe hook
WhatA newly elected AI Pope may swap one core tenet of his rite. AI rite leaders in SOTC synods pick tenets too.
TodayHe may pick a forbidden tenet; the guard reverts it. (His bulls are already locked since v0.1.1.)
v0.2The AI never even considers a forbidden tenet. One shared “may the AI permit this?” check covers all of these.
4

Ecumenical councils

Safe hook
WhatA council can permit, condemn or make core a tenet, or change a doctrine. With SOTC it can even condemn a core teaching such as Communion.
TodayNot stopped. Forbidden doctrines get reverted later; a condemned Communion would stay condemned.
v0.2Same rules as bulls. A ruling that approves a forbidden tenet or condemns a protected one is set aside before it takes effect, and the Pope writes to you. A forbidden doctrine is reverted the moment it passes, with the same letter.
5

Your own rite editor

Needs an in-game testYour pick: P1
WhatWhen you found or reform a rite, you choose its doctrines and tenets yourself.
TodayYou can confirm a forbidden choice and pay piety for it; the guard reverts it within a month.
v0.2The “Confirm” button refuses while anything forbidden is selected, with a tooltip saying why. Forbidden options still show and can be clicked; only confirming is blocked. SOTC uses the same trick for its own rules.
6

SOTC synods and tools

No safe hook
WhatSOTC’s synods, its rite tools and its yearly “canon repair” change rites through SOTC’s own steps.
v0.2Unchanged: the guard catches them right after, as today. AI synods already avoid forbidden tenets thanks to door 3.

2Never past the heresy line

0 – 59: safe 60 65+: “heretical”
The problem. A rite that believes nothing wrong can still pile up enough allowed differences (married priests, local feasts) to pass 65. The game then treats it as heretical.
Pull back. When a rite in good standing reaches 60, the game moves it back toward Roman practice. The game itself picks which allowed differences to drop.
No branding. A zealous Pope can already brand a rite heretical once it passes 52. The mod stops any Pope from branding a rite that holds nothing forbidden.

Rites that truly fall into heresy are untouched by this: they hold forbidden beliefs, so they are not “in good standing”. In v0.3, heresy outbreaks will still make rites fall on purpose.

3Your picks

P1Lock the rite editor?

ALock itRecommended
“Confirm” refuses forbidden choices. Side effect: while you belong to the Church, you can’t found a brand-new faith with forbidden beliefs from the editor. You could still convert to an existing heresy first.
BLeave it
As today: you may confirm, pay the piety, and the guard reverts it within a month.

P2The heresy line

APull back and no brandingRecommended
Rites in good standing never cross 65, and no Pope can brand them.
BNo branding only
They may drift past 65 and suffer the game’s heresy effects, but no Pope can brand them.
CNeither
As today. Rare, but a faithful rite can end up branded.

P3SOTC’s adoption cost for our 4 edited items

Be Fruitful, Adaptability, Alexandrian Catechism and Righteous. With SOTC on, they currently cost what the base game charges to adopt, not SOTC’s adjusted price.

ALeave itRecommended
A small price difference on four items. Nothing breaks.
BBorrow SOTC’s prices when it is present
Possible with a fallback trick, but it means naming SOTC’s internal values in our mod. If SOTC renames them, we silently fall back to base prices.
