1Deferred from the review

Issues the review found. All six are now fixed in v0.1, at your request. Shadows of the Cross compatibility is automatic: one mod, no patch, and it works with or without SOTC.

A

Main rite before the schism

✓ Fixed in v0.1
Fixed byA game rule, “Pope as Head Before the Schism” (on by default), makes the Papacy head of the undivided Church at game start. After the schism the old faith gives the title up, and the game makes the Pope head of Catholicism. If the schism is prevented, Rome keeps the primacy: if another see is seated, the Papacy is restored and players are told.
The issueBefore the schism the undivided Church has no head of faith in the game. So every month it makes the rite with the most counties (and at least as many holy sites) the main rite. That can be the Byzantine rite.
MeanwhileThe main rite may flip to Byzantine if the East grows larger. A newly elected non-Roman Pope’s old rite can also stay main for a short while. The guard is not affected: it protects every rite of the faith that holds the Roman rite, whichever one is main.
The fixMake the Pope head of faith before the schism. The main rite then always follows him, and he is always Roman. It comes behind a game rule that’s on by default.
B

Be Fruitful and Multiply’s divorce loophole

✓ Fixed in v0.1
Fixed byThe loophole is closed. A Church rite with this tenet always uses “Must be approved” for divorce, so every divorce, including one for low fertility, needs the Church’s approval (a bishop or the Pope). Within the Church, each parent also gains 50 piety for each child born of their marriage (not of concubines). The tenet keeps all its other effects: +25% fertility, +15% levies and reinforcement, easier spouse romance, health bonuses at birth, and faster temple growth. Outside the Church it works as in the base game.
The issueThis tenet lets a ruler divorce a spouse who can’t have children, even where divorce is Disallowed. You approved the tenet only without that effect.
MeanwhileNo Church rite starts with it as a core tenet. Even with it, the loophole only matters under “Disallowed”. Under the default “Must be approved”, the Pope still has to approve the divorce.
Why laterRemoving the effect means replacing the tenet’s whole definition. That belongs with the lock work, which replaces definitions anyway, and with the SOTC patch, since SOTC also redefines tenets.
C

Shadows of the Cross: “canon” tenets

✓ Fixed in v0.1
Fixed byAutomatic. When the guard swaps a tenet, it also updates SOTC’s canon record on that rite, so SOTC keeps the swap. Without SOTC there’s no record, and nothing happens.
The issueSOTC marks two tenets per rite as “canon” and puts them back if they’re removed. If a canon tenet were forbidden, the guard and SOTC would undo each other over and over.
MeanwhileI checked: no Church rite starts with a forbidden canon tenet. Only a rite founded later could hit this. If you ever see “The Faith Is Upheld” repeating every month for the same rite, this is the cause.
The fixA small SOTC patch that updates SOTC’s canon record whenever the guard swaps the tenet.
D

Shadows of the Cross: its heretical tenets by name

✓ Fixed in v0.1
Fixed byNot needed by name. The mod removes any tenet the Church prohibits that isn’t on your approved list, and SOTC prohibits all three in every Christian faith.
The issueSOTC adds three heretical tenets: Homoian Creed, Faultless Clergy and Free Will. Naming them in the base mod would cause errors for players without SOTC.
MeanwhileThey’re already caught. The Church prohibits them and they’re not on your approved list, so the guard removes them. The patch would only name them explicitly.
E

Mozarabic: Adaptability next to Righteous

✓ Fixed in v0.1
Fixed byWithin the Church, Adaptability now works with Righteous too. Fundamentalist still excludes it.
The issueThe start-of-game cleanup changes Mozarabic from Pluralist to Righteous, but it keeps the Adaptability tenet, which you approved. The game’s own rule only lets Pluralist rites pick Adaptability.
MeanwhileThe game doesn’t re-check tenets a rite already holds, so nothing breaks. It only matters if someone tries to pick Adaptability again later.
The fixWhen the lock replaces tenet definitions, add an exception so Adaptability fits a Righteous rite. That’s my lean. The other choice is to swap Mozarabic’s Adaptability for another tenet.
F

Two messages per correction

✓ Fixed in v0.1
Fixed byThe mod no longer sends its own message, so you only get the game’s “Rite updated”. A one-time notice at game start explains why such changes happen.
The issueWhen the guard fixes your rite, the game sends its own “Rite updated” message, and the mod adds “The Faith Is Upheld”.
MeanwhileYou get two messages for one fix. Ours explains why; the game’s shows what changed.
The fixDecide after you see it in play: keep both, or merge them into one.

2Planned in the design, not built yet

Parts of the approved design that come in later versions.

G

The lock

v0.2Visible
What it doesForbidden options disappear from councils, synods, the rite editor and AI drift.
MeanwhileThose can still pick forbidden things, and the guard undoes them within days. Example: a council “passes” divorce allowed, then a few days later the guard reverts it and you get a message.
H

Protected tenets and the Pope’s bulls

✓ Bulls fixed in v0.1.1
What it doesNo Pope can ever Prohibit the 8 protected tenets, such as Transubstantiation, or declare a forbidden tenet, such as Literalism, “in good standing”.
Fixed byWithin the Church, the bull and decision checks refuse both. Shadows of the Cross’s papal demands skip those checks, so a backstop catches them: the Pope sets the bull aside, the tenet’s status is restored, and the faithful get his letter instead of the usual one.
Still openEcumenical councils are not covered yet. They come with the lock (G) in v0.2.
I

Rule 2: never past the heresy line

v0.2Small
What it doesA rite in good standing may never pass 65 points, even through allowed differences like married priests.
Meanwhilev0.1 only removes forbidden beliefs. A rite could still pile up enough allowed differences to pass 65. The game would then call it heretical, and the Pope could cut it off, even though it believes nothing wrong. This should be rare.
J

Pope as head before the schism

✓ Fixed in v0.1
What it doesThe fix for item A. It also lets the Pope approve divorces (“Must be approved”) and use his powers before the schism.
MeanwhileSame as the base game: no head of faith until the schism.
K

Heresy features

v0.3Small
What it doesKind 1 outbreaks aim at Tier 2 rites, outbreaks come about twice as often, fallen rites get a signature heresy that carries them past the line, and they can return when they drop every error.
MeanwhileOutbreaks work as in the base game, with two differences. A kind 2 outbreak rite is marked fallen and left alone, but it stays inside the Church below the line until v0.3. A kind 1 push on a rite in good standing gets undone by the guard instead of making it fall.
L

Mending fix

v0.3None
What it doesEvery reunion after the schism happens with Rome. Only Catholic and Orthodox rulers can mend.
MeanwhileBase game behavior. An Orthodox or other Christian ruler who mends the schism could fold Catholicism into their own faith. That’s rare: it needs the schism to happen first, then a specific decision.
M

Seek Communion: a true return

v0.3None
What it doesA heresy the Pope takes back gives up its errors and rejoins as Tier 2.
MeanwhileBase game behavior: a heresy can regain friendly status while keeping its errors.
