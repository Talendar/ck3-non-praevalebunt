Design recommendation · CK3 1.21 + By God Alone

Non Praevalebunt

Catholic Doctrine Guard

“…et portae inferi non praevalebunt adversum eam.”Matthew 16:18

The Church never changes. Parts of the flock can fall away.

The Roman rite stands for Peter’s see, so its doctrine can never change. Other rites can fall into heresy, but only in ways history allows.

4An 867 start, over time

867 · One Church

The whole undivided Church is guarded: Roman (main), Insular, Mozarabic, Ambrosian, Beneventan, Carolingian, Byzantine, and almost surely Georgian.

885 · Slavic rite

The Slavic rite joins and is guarded.

Great Schism

The date depends on play. The Byzantine side leaves for Orthodoxy and is no longer guarded. Catholicism stays guarded.

If it’s mended

Any reunion is with Rome: Orthodox rites rejoin Catholicism, guarded again and cleaned. Only Catholics and Orthodox can mend it. Details →

1066 and 1178 starts: Catholicism begins with Roman (main), Insular, Mozarabic and Ambrosian. Guarding the Byzantine rite doesn’t block the schism, because the schism is driven by quarrels between the two sides, not by beliefs.

5How it’s built

Lock
Adds the rule “Catholic rites can’t pick this” to every forbidden doctrine and tenet.Blocks drift, new rites, councils, synods and the rite editor at the source.
Needs a small SOTC patch
Guard
Watches every rite change, plus a yearly check. Undoes forbidden picks and crossings of the heresy line. Skips Tier 3.Catches anything that slips past the lock, including other mods.
Works with most mods
Rules
Cleans the starting rites. Makes each Pope take the Roman rite. Marks fallen rites using the game’s own outbreak traces and gives each one a signature heretical tenet. Marks every heretical tenet Prohibited in the Catholic faith. Points kind 1 at Tier 2 and makes outbreaks more frequent. Keeps the Tier 1 list.No rewriting of the game’s outbreak events.
Works with most mods
