1The new messages (AN1)

Rewritten with some roleplay and a short catechesis. Any future message will follow the same style.

Upon This Rock
Once, at game start

“Thou art Peter; and upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.” (Matthew 16:18)

Christ promised that His Church would never teach error in faith or morals. Her rites may differ in custom and discipline, but never in what she believes. When a rite strays, the Church calls it back, and you will see it in your rite updates.

The Keys of Peter
If another see is made head before the schism

Some would set another see over the whole Church. Yet Christ gave the keys of the kingdom to Peter (Matthew 16:19), and they pass to his successor, the Bishop of Rome. The Pope remains head of faith of the undivided Church.

Fine: the other 27 tenets pair a real virtue with a real vice. For example, Confession admires honesty and despises deceit, and Purgatory admires forgiveness and despises vengeance. Cost of each fix: the mod has to replace the tenet’s whole definition, so the change also applies to non-Catholic faiths that hold it. Shadows of the Cross users also lose SOTC’s optional price rule for that tenet. That’s why I’d fix only the three clear clashes.

3Be Fruitful: other downside options (AN4)

“Chaste” stops being a sin in every option. All of the tenet’s other effects stay.

A · Small tax cost MY LEAN

Counties pay −5% tax, for the cost of raising and providing for large families.

+Ongoing and easy to understand. It’s the same kind of cost as Charity’s −3% tax.−Any lord with this tenet earns a little less.

B · Higher price to adopt

The tenet costs more piety to make core.

+No ongoing penalty.−Paid once, so it’s barely felt afterwards. It also doesn’t touch rites that start with it.

C · A different sin: callous

Followers despise coldness and cruelty toward one’s own.

+Keeps the usual “virtue and sin” shape.−Weak link to the tenet’s theme, and Love Thy Neighbour already uses this sin.

D · No downside, drop the piety bonus

No sin, no cost, and no piety for children.

+Simplest. Close to the base game’s strength.−A tenet with no trade-off at all.
Virtues, sins and penalties were read from the CK3 1.21.0.3 and By God Alone tenet files. Scripture is quoted from the Douay-Rheims.
