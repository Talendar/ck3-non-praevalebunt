Rites founded during play

They follow the rite they came from. A rite founded from the Roman rite goes to Catholicism, one from the Byzantine rite goes to Orthodoxy. The game does this on its own.

Which side counts as “the Church”

The game gives that role to whichever rite is the main one at the moment of the split. Our rule that every Pope takes the Roman rite makes that always Rome.

Struck-through rites are retired by the game at the split. ? = to confirm in the game.

2If the schism is prevented

The game has a “Prevent the Great Schism” decision. If someone takes it, the Church stays one, and the mod simply keeps guarding the undivided Church, Byzantine rite included. Nothing extra to build.

3If the schism is mended ✓ APPROVED
