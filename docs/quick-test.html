1Before you start (once)

Steam: turn on debug mode

Right-click Crusader Kings III›Properties›General›Launch options

Type -debug_mode in the box. It is empty today, so nothing else is needed.

This unlocks the console. Remove it later if you like.

Launcher: turn on the mod

Open your playset and add Non Praevalebunt. It is already in the mod list.

Keep your usual mods on, SOTC included. Put ours last.

If it is not in the list, restart the launcher.

2In the game

1
You doStart a new game in 867. Pick any Christian ruler in Western Europe, for example a count in Francia or Italy.
You seeA message called “Upon This Rock” on the first day.
2
You doOpen your faith’s window.
You seeThe Pope is the head of the faith.
3
You doOpen the console: the key under Esc, left of 1. Paste this line, press Enter, then close the console. effect rite:roman_rite = { change_rite_doctrine = doctrine_divorce_allowed }
You seeNothing yet. This plants an error on purpose: “divorce allowed” in the Roman rite.
4
You doUnpause and play at top speed for about 2 months.
You seeThe guard has put the Roman rite back to divorce needs approval. If you can’t find it in the faith window, don’t worry: the log tells me.
5
You doQuit to desktop, so the game finishes writing its logs. Then tell me “done”.
You seeNothing more. No need to save.

3What I check afterwards

error.log Any error from our files. Errors from your other mods are ignored.
game.log Our own notes: the Pope made head of the Church, the start-of-game fixes (Mozarabic), and the guard undoing the console test.

You don’t need to check the Mozarabic rite yourself. The log shows whether it was fixed.

If the game crashes, the mod is missing from the launcher, or a step looks wrong, stop there and tell me what you saw. A screenshot helps.
