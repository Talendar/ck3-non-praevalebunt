1Your calls: all 15 decided

#ItemMy leanWhy
1Uncle–niece marriage ★Forbid✓ DecidedCanon law banned it. Dispensations were very rare, and most came centuries later. It’s the game’s default, so forbidding it makes Catholic marriage stricter from day one.
2Cousin marriageAllow✓ DecidedFormally banned, but papal dispensations for royal cousins were common.
3Usury “Discouraged”Forbid✓ DecidedIts text says lending at interest is “no crime”. The Council of Vienne (1311) called it heresy to deny that usury is a sin.
4Alcohol ForbiddenForbid✓ DecidedWine is needed for Mass. Banning it outright was a mark of the Manichaeans.
5Diet: Fish AllowedForbid✓ DecidedNo meat ever, but fish allowed. That’s very close to what the Cathar elite ate.
6Funeral: Family RitesForbid✓ DecidedIts text describes ancestor veneration by rank, not Christian prayer for the dead.
7Gender viewForbid Female-dominated
Allow Equal✓ Decided		Female-dominated bars men from holding any title, which no Christian society ever did. Equal only changes politics. Clergy, bishops and the Pope stay male under a separate, locked doctrine.
8Be Fruitful and MultiplyAllow, edited✓ DecidedThe teaching is Catholic, but the game lets you divorce an infertile spouse. Allowed. Within the Church its low-fertility divorce needs the Church’s approval like any other, and parents gain piety for each child born of their marriage.
9The PentarchyBefore the schism only✓ DecidedThe Byzantine rite held it inside the undivided Church. The game itself prohibits it in Catholicism after 1054.
10Communal PossessionsAllow✓ DecidedShared goods, as in Acts 4:32. It was only heretical when made a duty for all.
11Alexandrian CatechismAllow✓ DecidedReason can know the moral law, which is Catholic natural law. Odd side effect: it makes “zealous” a sin.
12PacifismForbid✓ DecidedRejects just war entirely. Waldensians and Cathars held this.
13LiteralismForbid✓ DecidedScripture “needs no interpretation”, which rejects the Church’s teaching authority.
14AstrologyForbid✓ DecidedDivining the future from the stars was condemned.
15EsotericismForbid✓ DecidedOccult knowledge and alchemy.
