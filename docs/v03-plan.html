v0.3: Living Heresies

Heresies arise, take a name, are condemned, and can come home

← The approved heresy plan
1 · OutbreakA minor rite is hitOutbreaks come about twice as often, and hit a minor rite first.
2 · FallIt fallsThe mod marks it fallen. It is no longer guarded.
3 · SignatureIt takes a nameOne classic heresy, fitting its place and era, carries it past the line.
4 · CondemnationRome condemns itIt becomes a faith of its own, outside the Church.
5 · ReturnIt may come homeThrough Seek Communion, on the Church’s terms.

1What gets built

1

Aimed outbreaks

Safe hook

A light outbreak always hits a minor (Tier 2) rite if one exists, and that rite falls. Only if none exists does it hit a historic rite, which only changes customs and never falls.

2

More outbreaks

Safe hook

For the Church only, roughly twice as many:

  • Fervor bounce after an outbreak halved: +60/+75/+90 becomes +30/+40/+50.
  • Monthly odds doubled while fervor is 40 or less.

The five-year pause between outbreaks stays. The game’s own frequency rule still applies on top.

3

A signature heresy

Safe hook

A fallen rite takes one classic heresy as a core belief, by place and era:

  • Iberia before 1000: Adoptionism
  • Near the East: Monophysitism
  • Balkans, Occitania: Gnosticism (the Bogomil and Cathar line)
  • Rich, worldly sees, and anywhere else: Simony

Every kind of outbreak counts: light, dangerous new rites, and historical heresies.

4

The rule stays true

Safe hook
  • A fallen rite back under the line, still in error, is pushed over again.
  • A fallen rite that drops every error comes back as a normal guarded rite.
  • Any forbidden tenet that shows up in the Church is marked Prohibited there. The classic ones already are.
5

Seek Communion: a true return

Safe hook

When the Pope accepts, the heresy’s rites rejoin the Church with every error swapped for the nearest allowed belief. Their leaders are absolved, and the heresy stops spreading. The emptied faith stays as a shell, as the base game already does when the Schism is mended.

6

Mending the Schism: always with Rome

Your pick: P2

Whoever mends it, Orthodoxy rejoins Catholicism under the Pope. An Orthodox mender becomes Catholic with his rite, as the Greeks did at Florence (1439).

2Three things the research found

i
A heresy can overshoot into a faith of its own.At 100 divergence the game splits a rite off into a new faith by itself, skipping the Pope’s condemnation. So the mod aims each fallen rite at 75 to 90. Which tenet the heresy replaces matters, and the mod measures before and after.
0–65: in the Churchtarget 75–90100
ii
The AI Pope rarely condemns.After the Schism, he only condemns a rite whose leader he personally dislikes. Before it, he decides about once every few decades. An uncondemned heresy drains fervor, and low fervor brings more outbreaks, so heresies could pile up. See P1.
iii
SOTC rewrites one of the two “Mend the Schism” decisions.We can’t add a faith check to that one without breaking the rule of no SOTC overlap. See P2.

3Your picks

P1Making sure Rome condemns

AA fallbackRecommended
If a fallen rite is still not condemned about 5 years after crossing the line, the Pope condemns it with the game’s own bull: same letters, same effects. This works before and after the Schism.
BMake him dislike heresiarchs
An opinion penalty so the AI Pope’s own logic picks fallen rites. Side effect: it also changes his relations with those leaders, such as marriages and alliances.
CLeave it to the game
Risk: heresies pile up and fervor spirals down.

P2Other faiths mending the Schism

The approved design blocked anyone but Catholic and Orthodox rulers from mending. The research shows a cleaner way to reach the same goal, that every reunion is with Rome.

AAlways reunite with RomeRecommended
Whoever mends, even a Miaphysite, Orthodoxy joins Catholicism, and the mender’s own faith is left alone. No one can fold the Church into another faith. The conquest route is also limited to Catholic and Orthodox rulers, since we can change that one.
BAlso block other faiths on the diplomatic route
Possible only indirectly: the decision would show an impossible “support needed” requirement. Confusing to read.

4To watch in testing

Heresy spiralMore outbreaks and slow condemnations could stack up. P1 A is the brake. Numbers can be tuned after play.
Return before the SchismIf a heresy returns before 1054, the game’s Schism code may leave its rites behind. The monthly sweep moves them into Catholicism.
A named heresy breaking out againIf a reconciled historical heresy (say, the Cathars) tries to break out again, the game may log an error. To check.
Orthodox AI mendingAn eligible AI Orthodox ruler may now take the decision and bring Orthodoxy into Rome. That matches Florence, but it could come early.

All hooks are base-game steps that SOTC calls but doesn’t rewrite. v0.3 loads into a running v0.2 campaign: rites that already fell get their signature heresy at the next monthly check.
